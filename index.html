inspiring the future.

Founded in the winter of 1984, Greenwood Public Library began as a modest two-room collection housed in a restored heritage brick building. Over four decades, our patrons have helped expand this facility into a modern 35,000-square-foot architectural landmark.

Our mission remains steadfast: to foster literacy, intellectual curiosity, and democratic open access to information. Whether you are researching local history, composing your debut novel, or searching for a quiet corner to read on a rainy afternoon, Greenwood provides a welcoming shelter.

Beyond our extensive print catalog, Greenwood offers state-of-the-art technological amenities including high-speed fiber internet, private podcast recording suites, multi-format micro-film scanners, and quiet study pods equipped with ergonomic workstations.

We take immense pride in serving as the social anchor of Greenwood Township. Every month, our halls host free children's story times, English language learning circles, technology workshops, and panel discussions featuring regional authors and researchers.
