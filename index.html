A sanctuary for thought and quiet research in an increasingly noisy world.

Founded with a mission to serve the civic good, Greenwood Public Library has evolved from a modest reading room into a modern sanctuary of knowledge. Our stacks house both centuries-old literature and the latest contemporary works across every domain of human inquiry.

Beyond books, Greenwood serves as a vibrant social anchor. Our light-filled spaces host daily student study sessions, non-profit community meetings, free computer classes, and quiet places of refuge for anyone seeking reflection.

We invite you to step through our doors, pull a book from the shelves, and experience the timeless comfort of shared learning.
